Groups and large furniture & \texttt{cluster}, \texttt{row}, \texttt{n\_in\_row}($n$), \texttt{middle}, \texttt{around}($o$), \texttt{all\_near}($o$), \texttt{either\_side\_of}($o$), \texttt{ends\_of}($o$), \texttt{far\_end\_of}($o$), \texttt{on\_structure}($o$) & The candidates that sit together apart from the rest; that form one row, or a row of exactly $n$; the middle of a line; those that surround $o$, are all near it or flank it; those at its two ends, at its end away from the room's centre, or within its footprint. \\

Directions from a standpoint & \texttt{leftmost}($v$), \texttt{rightmost}($v$), \texttt{nearest}($v$), \texttt{farthest}($v$), \texttt{middle}($v$), \texttt{nth\_left}($v$, $n$), \texttt{nth\_right}($v$, $n$), \texttt{nth\_front}($v$, $n$), \texttt{dir\_of}(side, $v$, $o$) & Seen from $v$, facing the candidates: the one at the left or right end, the nearest or farthest, the middle one, the $n$-th from the left, right or front; to the left, right, front or back of $o$, with an optional object that the viewer faces. \\

\addlinespace

Negation & \texttt{not}($p$) & The sentence says that the candidate does not have relation $p$. \\
