\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

% Drafted 11-17 from PAPER_CLAIMS_v1 §5 and D6.  Each limitation is a boundary we know of, not a restatement of §4--§6.
% Single run per arm: RUN_INFO.txt of both 3D-LLaVA arms, SEED 42; no repeats exist.  Zero-target: sec/3_pipeline.tex.
The evidence has edges.
Each model is trained once per arm, and we report no variance; 3DRS is tested on fewer sets than the other two; three architectures are not all architectures.
The benchmark is written in the rewrites' style and filtered by our own scorer, because too few original sentences pin down a target among distractors to build it from them; whether the lead extends to human-written sentences that say where, we cannot tell.
The scorer needs instance labels and a frontier model as its reader; writer, reader and GPT-6 are one model family, which we do not control, and only the annotators stand outside it.
We do not rewrite zero-target descriptions or touch question answering.

The multi-target result deserves a second look.
A model that reads \emph{the chair} as one chair is marked wrong wherever the label holds four, and neither corpus lets it do what a person would: say that the sentence fits several, and ask.
Whether training on originals and rewrites together teaches both conventions, and whether grounding should be allowed to abstain, we leave open.
