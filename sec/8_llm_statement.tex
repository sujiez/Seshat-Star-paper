\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
Large language models are part of the method studied in this paper and were also used in preparing it.
\emph{In the research.} The writer that rewrites each annotation and the reader that translates a sentence into predicates (\cref{sec:pipeline,sec:scorer}) are prompted GPT-5.5 models (OpenAI) accessed through an API; their prompts are described in the paper and its supplement.
The frontier-model experiments in \cref{sec:model} query GPT-6 through the same kind of API.
No LLM was used to fabricate, select or alter experimental results; every number in the paper is computed by scripts from stored model outputs.
\emph{In the code and experiments.} The code of the pipeline, the scorer and the analyses was written and revised with an LLM-based coding assistant (Claude, via the Cursor editor), which also launched and monitored training and evaluation runs, under the authors' direction and review.
\emph{In the writing.} The text, the figures and the figure scripts were drafted and revised with the same assistant, which also helped to search for related work; every reference was checked against its publisher's or arXiv record. Drafts of the manuscript were also given to an LLM assistant (Claude, Anthropic) for reviewer-style feedback, which informed revisions of the text, the framing of some claims and the choice of some analyses. The authors set the claims, checked every statement and number against the experimental records, and are responsible for the whole paper.
