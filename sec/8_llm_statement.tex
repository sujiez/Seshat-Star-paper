\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
Large language models are part of the method studied in this paper and were also used in preparing it.
\emph{In the research.} The writer that rewrites each annotation and the reader that translates a sentence into predicates (\cref{sec:pipeline,sec:scorer}) are prompted GPT-5.5 models accessed through an API; their prompts and outputs are described in the paper and released with the data.
The frontier-model experiments in \cref{sec:model} query GPT-6 through the same kind of API.
No LLM was used to fabricate, select or alter experimental results; every number in the paper is computed by the released scripts from the released outputs.
\emph{In the writing.} The text, figures and figure scripts were drafted and revised with an LLM-based coding and writing assistant (Claude, via the Cursor editor) under the authors' direction; the authors wrote the claims, checked every statement and number against the experimental records, and are responsible for the whole paper.
